Our Process

Discovery

We start with a conversation. Share your vision, budget, and objectives. This is our chance to understand your unique requirements.

Start Now

Proposal

Once we gather the details, we craft a tailored proposal outlining the concept, budget breakdown, menu curation, and timelines for your approval.

Planning

We dive into the details. From sourcing fresh seafood to coordinating decor and audiovisuals, our team handles the logistics so you can relax.

Coordination

On the big day, we are on-site to ensure flawless execution. From setup to tear-down, we handle every aspect so you can enjoy the moment.
