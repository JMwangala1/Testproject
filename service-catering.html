Catering Services

Get in Touch

At Saint-Man Events, our culinary success is driven by our dedicated team of professional chefs who are passionate about delivering exceptional flavors. We curate specific menus tailored to your unique needs, ensuring that every bite is a memorable experience. Whether it is an intimate gathering or a grand corporate gala, we bring the restaurant experience to you.

Wedding Catering

Your wedding day deserves nothing short of perfection — and that includes the dining experience. At Saint-Man Events, we design bespoke wedding menus that reflect your taste, culture, and vision.

From elegant plated dinners to lavish buffet spreads and live cooking stations, we curate a culinary journey your guests will remember long after the last dance. Our team handles tasting sessions, presentation styling, professional waitstaff, and full service coordination, ensuring every plate served matches the beauty and significance of your special day.

Corporate Lunches

Impress clients, reward employees, or power productive meetings with our executive corporate catering services. We provide well-presented, timely, and professionally managed catering solutions tailored for board meetings, conferences, and office events.

Whether it’s gourmet lunch platters, executive buffets, or customized dietary menus, we ensure quality, punctuality, and polished service. Saint-Man Events understands corporate standards — and we deliver meals that enhance your brand image while keeping your guests satisfied and energized.

Private Parties

From birthdays and anniversaries to graduation celebrations and intimate family gatherings, we bring flavor and flair to your private events. Our team works closely with you to create menus that match your theme and guest preferences —

whether it’s traditional Kenyan cuisine, continental dishes, or fusion experiences. We manage everything from food preparation and presentation to service staff and cleanup, allowing you to relax and enjoy your celebration while we take care of the hospitality.

Outdoor & Festivals

Large-scale outdoor events require expertise, efficiency, and flawless logistics — and that’s where we excel. Whether it’s a community festival, corporate outdoor function, or major public event, we provide structured catering solutions that maintain quality even at high volumes.

Our team ensures hygienic food preparation, efficient service flow, and reliable setup in diverse environments. With Saint-Man Events, you get dependable catering that blends organization, taste, and professionalism — no matter the size of the crowd.

Private Chefs

Enjoy a luxury dining experience in the comfort of your home, villa, or private venue with our professional Private Chef services. At Saint-Man Events & Catering, we design customized menus tailored to your occasion — whether it’s an intimate dinner, birthday celebration, corporate hosting, or exclusive gathering.

From ingredient sourcing and preparation to elegant plating and clean-up, our chefs handle everything with precision and professionalism. Simply share your event details, approve your curated menu, and allow us to deliver a seamless, restaurant-quality experience directly to you.
